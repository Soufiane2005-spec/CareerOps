# CareerOps — Business Problem

## 1. Background

Students, recent graduates, and junior technology professionals
often search for internships and entry-level jobs across many
different platforms.

Opportunities are fragmented across job boards, company career
pages, university platforms, and other authorized sources.

As a result, candidates spend significant time repeatedly searching
for relevant opportunities and manually organizing their applications.

## 2. Main Problems

### 2.1 Fragmented Job Discovery

Relevant internships and junior jobs are distributed across many
different platforms.

Candidates must manually search multiple sources to discover new
opportunities.

### 2.2 Difficulty Evaluating Opportunities

Candidates may find an opportunity but have difficulty determining
whether their current skills match the job requirements.

They also lack a clear view of which skills appear most frequently
across relevant opportunities.

### 2.3 Skill-Gap Visibility

Candidates often know what they have learned but do not know which
missing skills would provide the greatest improvement to their job
prospects.

CareerOps will compare user skills against real job requirements to
identify frequently requested missing skills.

### 2.4 Application Tracking

Candidates frequently manage applications using browser bookmarks,
notes, spreadsheets, or memory.

This makes it difficult to track:

- where they applied
- application dates
- application status
- CV version used
- interviews
- follow-up dates
- offers and rejections

### 2.5 Follow-Up Management

Candidates can forget application deadlines, interview dates, or
follow-up opportunities.

CareerOps will provide reminders and status tracking to reduce this
problem.

## 3. CareerOps Solution

CareerOps will provide a centralized career intelligence platform
that:

- automatically imports opportunities from authorized job sources
- normalizes opportunity data
- matches job requirements with user skills
- calculates opportunity match scores
- identifies missing skills
- analyzes skill demand across available opportunities
- tracks applications
- manages CV versions
- manages follow-up and interview reminders

## 4. Expected User Benefit

CareerOps should help users:

- spend less time searching multiple job platforms
- discover more relevant opportunities
- prioritize opportunities with higher skill compatibility
- understand which skills to learn next
- organize their job search
- avoid missing important application follow-ups
